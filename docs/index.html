Everything you need

One app for your
money, goals, and debts.

Designed for the way Filipinos actually budget — multi-wallet, paluwagan-aware, and private by default.

💳

Multi-wallet tracking

BDO, GCash, Maya, cash, crypto, passbook — every wallet in your life, organised by category and color, with live net worth.

🔁

Atomic transfers

Move money between wallets in one tap. PesoFlow writes a paired in/out transaction so balances always match — and deletes both sides if you undo.

🎯

Ipon goals

Motor, Emergency Fund, Japan Trip — set targets, watch the savings jar fill up with a wavy liquid + floating coins, and get an ETA from your saving habits.

🤝

Debt tracker

Money owed to you and money you owe — with partial payments, due dates, and color-coded "Paid / On track / Due soon / Overdue" status.

🔍

Smart search

Find any wallet, goal, debt, or transaction by name, person, category or note. Results group by type with one-tap navigation.

📅

Day · Month · Year filters

Slice your transaction history by today, a specific month, an entire year, or a custom date range. Income, expense, and net are recomputed instantly.

🛡️

Secret vault

Hidden wallets unlocked by a separate PIN. Vault balances are excluded from your dashboard and net worth — only you know they exist.

👁️

Hide balances

One tap on the eye icon replaces every amount in the app with ••••••. Perfect for showing your screen to a friend without exposing your balance.

📲

QR codes & account numbers — all in one place

Save your GCash, Maya, BDO QR codes once and keep them inside PesoFlow. Pick from gallery, crop to a perfect square, then tap any wallet to expand its QR fullscreen for someone to scan. Account numbers copy to clipboard with a single tap. No more digging through banking apps just to receive money.

FAQ

Things people ask.

Is PesoFlow really free?

Yes — PesoFlow is a personal project by Michael Flora, free to use and free to share. There are no ads, no subscriptions, and no in-app purchases.

Where is my data stored?

Locally on your device, in an encrypted Hive database inside the app's private storage. No data is sent to any server — there isn't even a server to send it to.

What if I lose my phone?

Your data is gone with the device. That's why the Backup feature in Settings exists — export a JSON file periodically and store it somewhere safe (Google Drive, your computer, anywhere).

Can I use it on iOS?

The codebase supports iOS, but only the Android APK is published right now. iOS distribution requires an Apple Developer account.

What is the Secret Vault?

A separate space inside PesoFlow protected by its own PIN (different from the main unlock PIN). Wallets in the vault are excluded from your dashboard, totals, and net worth — only visible after entering the vault PIN.

How do I install the APK?

Download the APK to your Android phone, tap it in your file manager. Android will ask you to allow "Install unknown apps" once for your browser/file manager — that's normal for any app installed outside the Play Store.
