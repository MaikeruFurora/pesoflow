# PesoFlow

> Save smart. Stay secure.

PesoFlow is a private, on-device Filipino savings app that helps you track every peso across your wallets, ipon goals, and debts — without an account, without the cloud.
 
---

## ✨ Features

### 💳 Multi-wallet tracking
Track all your money in one place:

- BDO
- GCash
- Maya
- Cash
- Crypto
- Passbook accounts

PesoFlow automatically computes your real-time net worth.

---

### 🔁 Atomic transfers
Move money between wallets safely.

PesoFlow creates paired transactions automatically so balances always stay correct — even when editing or deleting transfers.

---

### 🎯 Ipon goals
Create savings goals like:

- Emergency Fund
- Motorcycle
- Japan Trip
- New Laptop

Watch your savings jar fill up with animated liquid waves and floating coins.

---

### 🤝 Debt tracker
Track:

- Money owed to you
- Money you owe

Features include:

- Partial payments
- Due dates
- Status tracking
- Overdue indicators

---

### 🔍 Smart search
Instantly search:

- Wallets
- Goals
- Debts
- Transactions
- Notes
- People

---

### 📅 Flexible transaction filters
View transactions by:

- Day
- Month
- Year
- Custom date range

Income, expenses, and totals update instantly.

---

### 🛡️ Secret vault
Hide private wallets behind a separate PIN.

Vault balances are excluded from your dashboard and net worth.

---

### 👁️ Hide balances
Tap the eye icon to instantly hide every amount on screen.

Perfect when showing your phone to someone else.

---

### 📲 QR codes & account numbers
Store your payment QR codes directly inside PesoFlow.

Supports:

- GCash QR
- Maya QR
- Bank QR
- Account numbers

Quick fullscreen QR display for easier receiving of payments.

---

## 🔒 Privacy First

PesoFlow is built with privacy by default.

### 🔐 6-digit PIN
Your app is protected using a secure PIN system.

### 👆 Biometric unlock
Supports fingerprint and Face ID authentication.

### ☁️ No cloud
No accounts. No servers. No tracking. No telemetry.

### 📤 Offline backup
Export your own JSON backup anytime.

Your data stays yours.
